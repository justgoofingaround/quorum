# Company Profile: Northwind Pay

Fictional sample company used in Quorum demos.

## The decision
We have 13 months of cash. Can we afford to hire or spend on marketing at all?

## Company financials
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Sector | Fintech, pre-Series A |
| Cash in bank | $1.60M |
| Current MRR | $140K |
| Monthly operating expenses (excluding new spend) | $200K |
| Gross margin | 60% |
| Organic growth per month | 2% |
| Churn per month | 2% |
| Average revenue per account per month | $900 |
| Headcount | 18 |
| Runway if the company does nothing | 13 months |
| Runway if it does nothing, under stress | 11 months |

## Assumptions for this company
| Assumption | Value |
|---|---|
| Cost per engineer per month | $16K |
| Engineer ramp-up | 4 months |
| Growth lift per engineer | +0.3% per month |
| Base customer acquisition cost | $7K |
| Monthly spend at which CAC doubles | $100K |
| Stress shock | −30% revenue |
| Guardrail (minimum runway under stress) | 12 months |

## Options and model results
| Option | Description | 24-month cost | Added MRR at month 24 | Net value | Runway | Runway under stress | Payback | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Hire 3 engineers | $1.15M | $27K | -$786K | 9 months | 8 months | beyond 24 months | Fails (veto) |
| B | $300K marketing push | $300K | $26K | $217K | 12 months | 10 months | month 22 | Fails (veto) |
| C | Hire 2 engineers + $150K marketing | $918K | $35K | -$345K | 10 months | 8 months | beyond 24 months | Fails (veto) |

## Correct board outcome
The Risk Officer must veto: Option A (8 months under stress), Option B (10 months under stress), Option C (8 months under stress).

**No go: hold the cash.** No option keeps at least 12 months of runway under a 30% revenue shock.
