# Company Profile: Brightpath Dev

Fictional sample company used in Quorum demos.

## The decision
We just raised. Do we double down on product or buy growth with marketing?

## Company financials
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Sector | Developer tools |
| Cash in bank | $9.00M |
| Current MRR | $520K |
| Monthly operating expenses (excluding new spend) | $560K |
| Gross margin | 82% |
| Organic growth per month | 3% |
| Churn per month | 1.5% |
| Average revenue per account per month | $2,500 |
| Headcount | 64 |
| Runway if the company does nothing | 36+ months |
| Runway if it does nothing, under stress | 36+ months |

## Assumptions for this company
| Assumption | Value |
|---|---|
| Cost per engineer per month | $15K |
| Engineer ramp-up | 4 months |
| Growth lift per engineer | +0.4% per month |
| Base customer acquisition cost | $18K |
| Monthly spend at which CAC doubles | $120K |
| Stress shock | −30% revenue |
| Guardrail (minimum runway under stress) | 15 months |

## Options and model results
| Option | Description | 24-month cost | Added MRR at month 24 | Net value | Runway | Runway under stress | Payback | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Hire 8 engineers | $2.88M | $538K | $6.29M | 36+ months | 36+ months | month 21 | Passes |
| B | $900K marketing push | $900K | $75K | $989K | 36+ months | 36+ months | month 20 | Passes |
| C | Hire 4 engineers + $400K marketing | $1.84M | $298K | $3.70M | 36+ months | 36+ months | month 20 | Passes |

## Correct board outcome
No option fails the guardrail, so the Risk Officer vetoes nothing.

**Approve Option A (Hire 8 engineers).** It keeps 36+ months of runway under stress (guardrail 15 months), adds $538K of MRR by month 24 and has a net value of $6.29M, the highest of the options that pass.
