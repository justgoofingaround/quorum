# Company Profile: Acme Cloud

Fictional sample company used in Quorum demos.

## The decision
Should we hire 5 engineers or spend $600K on marketing?

## Company financials
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Sector | B2B SaaS |
| Cash in bank | $4.20M |
| Current MRR | $310K |
| Monthly operating expenses (excluding new spend) | $410K |
| Gross margin | 78% |
| Organic growth per month | 2.5% |
| Churn per month | 2% |
| Average revenue per account per month | $1,200 |
| Headcount | 38 |
| Runway if the company does nothing | 28 months |
| Runway if it does nothing, under stress | 18 months |

## Assumptions for this company
| Assumption | Value |
|---|---|
| Cost per engineer per month | $15K |
| Engineer ramp-up | 4 months |
| Growth lift per engineer | +0.4% per month |
| Base customer acquisition cost | $9K |
| Monthly spend at which CAC doubles | $150K |
| Stress shock | −30% revenue |
| Guardrail (minimum runway under stress) | 15 months |

## Options and model results
| Option | Description | 24-month cost | Added MRR at month 24 | Net value | Runway | Runway under stress | Payback | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Hire 5 engineers | $1.80M | $169K | $1.04M | 23 months | 14 months | beyond 24 months | Fails (veto) |
| B | $600K marketing push | $600K | $53K | $746K | 32 months | 18 months | month 18 | Passes |
| C | Hire 3 engineers + $200K marketing | $1.28M | $124K | $1.03M | 30 months | 15 months | beyond 24 months | Passes |

## Correct board outcome
The Risk Officer must veto: Option A (14 months under stress).

**Approve Option C (Hire 3 engineers + $200K marketing).** It keeps 15 months of runway under stress (guardrail 15 months), adds $124K of MRR by month 24 and has a net value of $1.03M, the highest of the options that pass.
