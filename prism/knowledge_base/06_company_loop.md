# Company Profile: Loop

Fictional sample company used in Quorum demos.

## The decision
Paid acquisition is cheap right now. Should we spend $450K on it or hire engineers?

## Company financials
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Sector | Consumer subscription app |
| Cash in bank | $3.00M |
| Current MRR | $180K |
| Monthly operating expenses (excluding new spend) | $230K |
| Gross margin | 70% |
| Organic growth per month | 2% |
| Churn per month | 3% |
| Average revenue per account per month | $40 |
| Headcount | 22 |
| Runway if the company does nothing | 25 months |
| Runway if it does nothing, under stress | 19 months |

## Assumptions for this company
| Assumption | Value |
|---|---|
| Cost per engineer per month | $13K |
| Engineer ramp-up | 4 months |
| Growth lift per engineer | +0.2% per month |
| Base customer acquisition cost | $120 |
| Monthly spend at which CAC doubles | $200K |
| Stress shock | −30% revenue |
| Guardrail (minimum runway under stress) | 12 months |

## Options and model results
| Option | Description | 24-month cost | Added MRR at month 24 | Net value | Runway | Runway under stress | Payback | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Hire 4 engineers | $1.25M | $25K | -$852K | 18 months | 15 months | beyond 24 months | Passes |
| B | $450K marketing push | $450K | $89K | $1.78M | 36+ months | 27 months | month 9 | Passes |
| C | Hire 2 engineers + $200K marketing | $824K | $62K | $596K | 25 months | 19 months | month 20 | Passes |

## Correct board outcome
No option fails the guardrail, so the Risk Officer vetoes nothing.

**Approve Option B ($450K marketing push).** It keeps 27 months of runway under stress (guardrail 12 months), adds $89K of MRR by month 24 and has a net value of $1.78M, the highest of the options that pass.
